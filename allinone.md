## Summary

**Overview.** Four union queries covering HOV-SG, ConceptGraphs, Hydra, and the original 3D Scene Graph formulation, 2019–2026; 75 unique papers after cross-source dedup. The corpus spans the full arc of the field: the 2019–2022 foundations, the 2023–2024 open-vocabulary turn, and the 2025–2026 wave of applications (multi-robot, Gaussian-splatting scene graphs, LLM planning).

**Trends.** Volume surges from 2024 onward, driven by the open-vocabulary + LLM-planning combination. Dominant venues: ICRA/IROS/RSS/RA-L on the robotics side, CVPR/ICCV on the vision side. Recurring groups: MIT SPARK (Hughes, Chang, Carlone — Hydra [4,9,20,24,25]), the Freiburg group (Werby, Büchner, Burgard — HOV-SG [14], Collaborative Dynamic [2]), and Udugama/Vosselman/Nex (Mono-Hydra line [3,12,16,38]).

**Key themes.**
1. *Foundational hierarchical scene graphs* — the original formulation and real-time construction ([41], [9], [7]).
2. *Open-vocabulary scene graphs* — CLIP/VLM-derived semantics replacing closed label sets ([1], [33], [11]).
3. *Hierarchy for language-grounded navigation* — floors/rooms/objects structured for language queries ([14], [13], [50]).
4. *Scene graphs as LLM-planner interfaces* — graphs serialized/prompted for LLM task and path planning ([22], [35], [49]).
5. *Multi-robot and collaborative graphs* ([25], [6], [2]).
6. *Monocular / Gaussian-splat construction* ([12], [17], [42]).
